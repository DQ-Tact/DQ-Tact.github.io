Details
No. of Attacks Base Potency Damage Calc. Secondary Effect Chance
1 - - 100%:
Revives all fallen Undead allies to 100% of their max HP. The fallen allies will be revived in the spaces directly adjacent to the user. The revived allies will still be counted as an ally that has been KO'd, and so this effect cannot be used to bypass clear conditions that check for remaining allies (for example, if one ally is KO'd and then revived using this ability, the condition "Clear with fewer than 1 party member KO'd" would not be met)

100%:
Ally's ATK +1 (3 turns)
Ally's DEF +1 (3 turns)
Ally's AGL +1 (3 turns)
Ally's WIS +1 (3 turns)
Ally gains Undead March (3 turns)
